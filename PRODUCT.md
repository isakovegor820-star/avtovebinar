# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Registered webinar participants who confirm their email, enter the “Мои вебинары” account, and open the relevant scheduled room from its webinar card. Reminder and Telegram deep links may still open the room directly.

Secondary users are invited АСПБ managers and operators who need the chat, questions, registrations, partner applications, reminders, and CRM state to stay synchronized with the video timeline. External organizations cannot create their own workspaces.

## Product Purpose

The product turns a recorded АСПБ webinar into a managed scheduled event: registration, personal room access, server-based live timing, scripted timeline prompts, chat, questions, partner CTA, CRM capture, email reminders, and Telegram notifications.

Success means the room feels like a clear, reliable scheduled broadcast without pretending that prerecorded content is a real live conversation. Viewers should understand who АСПБ is, what a partner does, what АСПБ handles, how terms are fixed, and what next action to take.

The product is a single-owner АСПБ service. Tenant entities remain an internal authorization boundary and compatibility layer, not a public multi-organization product.

## Brand Personality

Expert, calm, practical.

The interface should feel trustworthy and operational: legal and financial topics need confidence, plain language, visible state, and low friction. It should avoid theatrical urgency, vague motivational copy, or manipulative live-chat behavior.

## Anti-references

Do not make the experience feel like a generic video archive, a fake live stream, a casino-style countdown funnel, or a heavy legal lecture.

Avoid pretending that scripted messages are real-time human activity. Avoid overpromising debt outcomes, guaranteed payouts, grey-scheme language, and date-specific phrases that will age inside a reusable recording.

## Design Principles

1. Make the scheduled-event state obvious: before, during, replay, and after-webinar states must be visually and textually unambiguous.
2. Keep the viewer oriented around the next action: watch, ask, review the current block, or leave a partner application.
3. Use chat to reduce hesitation, not to fake activity: scripted agent questions should feel like common participant questions tied to the next answer in the recording.
4. Preserve trust around legal and financial claims: show diagnostics, contracts, and role boundaries without exaggerated promises.
5. Optimize for completion: video playback, chat, CTA, and access recovery must remain stable on desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for the public room and registration path. Preserve keyboard access for video controls, forms, chat input, and CTAs. Maintain readable contrast for body text and muted labels, support reduced motion, and avoid relying on color alone for live, locked, replay, error, and CTA states.
